6.5 kg Dumbbell Training
Today's Strength Log
💪 Recommended Routine (2 × 6.5kg)
Push (Chest/Shoulders/Triceps)
Floor Dumbbell Press
3 × 12–15 reps
Keep core tight. Press explosively up, lower slowly (3s down). Builds pushing power for punches.
Pull (Back/Biceps)
Bent-over Row
3 × 10–12 reps each arm
Hinge at hips, back flat. Row elbow to hip. Mimics pulling guard breaks in kumite.
Shoulders
Arnold Press
3 × 10 reps
Start palms facing you, rotate out as you press. Builds shoulder stability for blocking.
Core (Rotation Power)
Russian Twist (weighted)
3 × 20 reps (10 per side)
Hold one dumbbell at chest. Rotate fully. This is your hip rotation power for kicks.
Legs
Goblet Squat
4 × 15 reps
Hold one dumbbell at chest. Deep squat, knees out. Builds stance stability and explosive takeoffs.

Crushing Strength
Gripper Reps + Holds

Forearm Flexors
Wrist Curl (supinated)
3 × 20 reps, light and slow
Rest forearms on knees/bench. Full range of motion. Don't go too heavy — quality reps.
Forearm Extensors
Reverse Wrist Curl
3 × 15 reps
Often neglected. Balances forearm strength and prevents injury. Crucial for blocking.
Compound Forearm
Zottman Curl
3 × 10 reps
Curl up with palms up, rotate to palms down on the way down. Hits biceps AND brachioradialis in one move.
